McMaster Mars Rover

Designed and manufactured a custom electrical enclosure for the McMaster Mars Rover to securely house and protect the rover's primary electrical systems. The enclosure was engineered to support 15+ electrical subsystems, including PCBs, power distribution hardware, NVIDIA Jetson AGX modules, Ethernet interfaces, cooling fans, battery components, LEDs, and onboard cameras while providing accessibility for maintenance and system integration.

Designed the complete enclosure in SolidWorks, creating the structural frame, acrylic side panels, mounting brackets, support members, fastener layouts, and internal component mounts. The design prioritized structural rigidity, ease of assembly, cable management, and compatibility with the rover chassis while meeting subsystem packaging constraints.

Performed Finite Element Analysis (FEA) to validate structural performance under expected loading conditions and completed airflow simulations to optimize cooling for heat-generating electronics. These analyses informed design revisions that improved thermal performance, reduced structural deflection, and increased overall system reliability.

Integrated wiring and electrical hardware throughout the enclosure, routing power and communication cables between components while ensuring serviceability and organized cable management. The enclosure is currently being manufactured and assembled for integration into the team's next-generation competition rover.

Embedded Assistive Robotics Device

Designed and developed an embedded electromechanical assistive device that automated a custom claw mechanism using an Arduino Uno, PWM-controlled servo motor, and single-button interface. The project focused on applying embedded systems, mechanical design, and electronics integration to create a practical solution that reduced physical effort through one-handed operation.

Final CAD Model of Embedded Assistive Device.

Designed the complete mechanical system in SolidWorks, including the telescoping structure, linkage mechanism, motor mounts, electronic enclosure, and structural supports. Manufactured the prototype using FDM 3D printing and integrated the embedded electronics, wiring, and mechanical components into a lightweight 0.379 kg system with a total material cost of $60.46.

Final Prototype in Collapsed Position due to Telescoping Mechanism.

Developed the embedded control software in Arduino C/C++, implementing PWM-based servo control, button state management, and timing algorithms to automate the claw mechanism. During testing, I refined the control strategy by transitioning from position-based to time-based servo control, improving motion consistency and overall system reliability.

Arduino C/C++ Code Used for Servo Motor PWM Control and Claw Movement.

Completed 12+ design iterations through rapid prototyping and system testing, optimizing mechanical tolerances, joint alignment, wire routing, and component integration. Reduced critical joint clearances from 0.5 mm to 0.2 mm, improving structural stability while maintaining smooth mechanical operation.
